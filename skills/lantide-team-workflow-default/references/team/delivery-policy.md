# Delivery policy

> Customization status: Default
> Safe to edit: Yes
> Required: Yes
> Purpose: Define what counts as a complete, reviewable analysis delivery.

## Default deliverables

- Use a **Markdown Report** for a durable decision-facing narrative with findings, evidence references, limitations, and an analysis appendix.
- Add a managed **HTML Report** when the audience needs a shareable web presentation, interactive charts, or a polished visual layer.
- Use a validated table or exported result only when the request is genuinely tabular and the receiving context does not require a narrative conclusion.
- A chat answer may summarize or navigate, but it is not the sole deliverable for formal analysis.

## Completion standard

A formal delivery is complete only when:

- it answers the approved question for the intended audience;
- material numbers can be traced to formal evidence;
- definitions, time range, grain, filters, and comparison basis are clear where relevant;
- limitations, uncertainty, missing data, and unresolved risks are visible;
- the artifact has passed the review required by `roles-and-review.md` and the live Lantide lifecycle;
- any external destination or delivery relationship is recorded through an approved mechanism;
- the Agent has read back the authoritative artifact before claiming what was delivered.

## Distribution

Before external distribution, confirm the exact artifact, audience, format, and destination. Do not export to an invented path, post to an unapproved system, or treat a local temporary file as the final managed deliverable.

## Team extensions

Organizations may define mandatory templates, branding, accessibility checks, approved destinations, retention labels, publication owners, or audience-specific review gates here.
