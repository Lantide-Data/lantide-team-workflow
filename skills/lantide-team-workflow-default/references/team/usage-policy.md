# Usage policy

> Customization status: Default
> Safe to edit: Yes
> Required: Yes
> Purpose: Decide when analysis must, should, or need not use Lantide.

## Must use Lantide

Use a formal Lantide workflow when any condition applies:

- a result may influence a business, product, operational, financial, or customer decision;
- the output cites real data, a metric, SQL, a statistical result, or a forecast;
- another person must review scope, definitions, methods, or conclusions;
- the work may need to be rerun, refreshed, audited, or explained later;
- the outcome will become a Report, dashboard, presentation, recurring analysis, or external delivery;
- multiple sources, transformations, or validation checks materially affect trust.

## Should use Lantide

Prefer Lantide when the task involves multi-step exploration, joins, reusable queries, intermediate results, statistical methods, comparisons across periods, or uncertainty that may later become a formal claim.

Start as exploration when the question or available data is genuinely uncertain. Move to a formal workflow before an exploratory result is cited, approved, distributed, or used as the basis for a decision.

## May remain outside Lantide

Lantide is optional for:

- a one-off arithmetic calculation with supplied values;
- a general SQL, statistics, or product concept question;
- a synthetic example that does not use organizational data;
- wording or brainstorming work with no analytical claim;
- a low-risk disposable task the user explicitly says does not need retention or review.

If an apparently small task produces a real organizational number or reusable conclusion, reclassify it.

## Classification output

Before substantive work, classify the request as one of:

- **Outside Lantide:** no durable analysis record is needed.
- **Lantide exploration:** investigate uncertainty, but do not present results as formal evidence.
- **Lantide formal analysis:** follow Plan, execution evidence, review, and delivery requirements.

State the reason briefly. Do not turn classification into ceremony when the choice is obvious.
