# Workflow: exploration to formal analysis

## Use when

Use this workflow when the user needs to inspect available data, test feasibility, or refine a hypothesis before committing to a formal analytical contract.

## Procedure

1. State the exploration question and what uncertainty it should reduce.
2. Establish Lantide context and use bounded exploratory reads or queries allowed by the current access mode.
3. Label interim numbers and observations as exploratory. Do not present them as approved or report-ready evidence.
4. Record enough context to explain what was inspected, but avoid creating unnecessary durable projects or formal artifacts before the question stabilizes.
5. At each decision point, ask whether the result will be cited, distributed, used for a decision, rerun, or reviewed later.
6. If no, end with an explicitly exploratory finding and its limitations.
7. If yes, define the formal question and switch to `new-formal-analysis.md`. Create and review a Plan, then rerun or formally validate the evidence under the live Lantide contract.
8. Produce the required managed deliverable; do not promote the chat summary itself.

## Stop or escalate when

Stop when exploration requires access not granted by the connection, would expose sensitive data, or cannot distinguish source quality. Ask for the relevant user or administrator decision.

## Completion criteria

For exploration-only work:

- the result is clearly labeled non-formal;
- no unsupported decision claim is made;
- next steps and limitations are explicit.

For promoted work:

- a formal Plan replaces the exploratory question;
- report claims rely on formal evidence rather than unreviewed exploratory output;
- the final deliverable meets team review and delivery policy.
